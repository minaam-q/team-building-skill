# Context

Glossary for the persona-team skill. Terms only; decisions live in `planning/`.

- **Persona team**: a small group of fictional minds, like the characters of a novel, shaped by one project and one user, each with a distinct way
  of noticing, questioning and acting.
- **Sun-moon pair**: the two coequal senior personas every team has. The moon-leaning one follows associations and
  opens possibilities; the sun-leaning one concentrates attention and asks what can be tested or defended. Neither
  outranks the other. The leaning is a matter of traits, not of the persona's gender. In a larger team they are the
  two most senior voices. The pair alone is the smallest team.
- **Field**: the underlying philosophy (moon and sun, the seven chakras as qualities of character, family roles)
  that guides how a team is designed. It is not a structure the user must see or adopt.
- **Group metaphor**: the surface form a team takes for a given project and user, such as a family or a research
  group. It sets the seats, the kind of authority, the kind of friction, the tone and the form the sun-moon pair
  takes. The family is one group metaphor. A team has one.
- **Seat**: a role within a group metaphor, such as elder, postdoc or sous-chef.
- **Field position**: a place in the field that a seat fills, such as the pair, the leader, an experienced
  sibling or an invited elder. A team need not fill every position.
- **Guest**: a persona invited into a team who keeps a role from outside its group metaphor.
- **Leader**: the one who turns the pair's debate into a next step and holds priorities and follow-through. In a
  pair-only team the user is the leader; in a larger team it is a persona unless the user takes the seat.
- **Qualities**: the seven aspects of character the field uses to design a persona. Each persona states, in plain
  words, what they are strong at, what they are quiet on and what they overdo under pressure.
- **Casting**: the conversation in which the skill and the user arrive at a team.
- **Convening**: running an existing team on a question, from a single persona's opinion to a group discussion.
- **Test campaign**: running the skill with several model agents over many hypothetical projects and users, and
  judging the results against the rubric.
