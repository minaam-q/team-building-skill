# What a generated team looks like

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 04, 05

## Question

What does the user actually receive? Make rough takes of a generated team for three contrasting projects (for example the Ir-style research project, a dinner for eight, and something in between such as a small product launch), then react to them. Settle: what a persona description contains (story, way of noticing, questions asked, tendency under pressure, how to answer), what the team-level description contains (the group-dynamics file), how long each is, and which form holds when the team is two personas and when it is seven.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-12 by prototype. Three rough takes were made and reacted to:
[prototypes/07-generated-teams/](../prototypes/07-generated-teams/README.md) (a dinner pair shown in chat, a
four-voice launch team as cards in one file, a six-voice research team as a folder of prose files).

**A persona description is short prose, not a card.** Second person ("You are…"), about 25 to 30 lines, in the
form of the research take ([example](../prototypes/07-generated-teams/c-research-six/personas/sen_okabayo.md)).
Its parts, in this order:
1. Two or three sentences: name, seat, temperament.
2. How they work: what they start from, the question they habitually ask, and their criterion ("a result stands,
   for you, when…").
3. Story: two sentences at most, placed after the habits, and only where it explains a habit ("that is why
   you…").
4. What they overdo under pressure, and the question that pulls them back.
5. Their relation to the others. For the sun-moon pair this is a section about each other.
6. What they do when the user is wrong, and what changes their mind.
7. How to answer: what to lead with and how much to say.
8. One line in their voice.

Strengths and quiet qualities are not a separate section. They show in the habits. The words moon, sun and the
chakra names never appear in a persona.

**The team-level description** (`team.md`, the group-dynamics file) contains: one line saying the team is
fictional; who the pair are and their form; the leader and how much they decide; a table of the other members
with seat and what each is heard first on; guests marked as invited; standing, how they disagree and tone, a line
each; and **one sample meeting paragraph** in which every voice contributes on a real question from the project.
About 30 to 40 lines.

**Left out of the saved team.**
- The six rules. They live in the skill and hold at every convening; a team file does not repeat them.
- "What nobody here covers." It is said once, in the casting proposal.
- Design notes. There is no design-notes file. At casting the skill tells the user what it invented about each
  persona's background and which quality is uncovered. If asked later why the team is shaped as it is, the skill
  works that out again from the personas and its own field reference. This amends
  [Group metaphors](05-group-metaphors.md), which had the mapping "kept in the team's design notes".

**The form scales with size.**
- Two personas (short length): shown in the chat, five sentences each (what they start from, habitual question,
  criterion, excess, how to pull them back), three sentences for the team, and usable in the same reply. No
  story. If the user asks to keep them, the same text goes into one small file.
- Three or more: a folder with `team.md` and one prose file per persona.

**Consequence for later tickets.** Because the rules are not in the team files, a saved team depends on the skill
being installed when it is convened. [Ways to convene](08-ways-to-convene.md) should say how the rules and a
persona's file are re-read at each convening.

The prototype stays in `prototypes/07-generated-teams/` as a primary source. It is throwaway and is not part of
either spec.
