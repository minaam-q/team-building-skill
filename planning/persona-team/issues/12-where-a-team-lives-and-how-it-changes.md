# Where a team lives and how it changes

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 07, 08, 13, 15

## Question

A saved team is a folder (`team.md` plus one file per persona), and convening now produces meeting minutes, raw
logs of long one-on-ones and their reviews (see [Ways to convene](08-ways-to-convene.md)). Settle: where the team
folder and these records sit (in the project, in a user-level place, or either), on each kind of host; how a team
carries across sessions and how the skill finds it again; how the user adds, retires or re-tunes a persona, or
changes the metaphor, after casting; how a short pair kept in chat becomes a saved team; and whether one team can
be reused across projects.

What is kept at each level of retention is settled in [What a team remembers](13-what-a-team-remembers.md);
this ticket places it. Note from the Ir project: its persona files exist in three identical copies (the skill,
`docs/agents/` and `docs/agents/research_group/`), so say which copy is the team and what happens to the others.
