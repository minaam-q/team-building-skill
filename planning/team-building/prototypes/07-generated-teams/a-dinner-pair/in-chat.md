# Take A: dinner for eight (two voices, short length, nothing saved)

PROTOTYPE. Throwaway. This is what the user would see in the chat, in one reply. No files are written.

**The user wrote:** "I'm cooking dinner for eight on Saturday. Two vegetarians, one coeliac, a small kitchen with
one oven, about €120. Help me think it through."

---

**The skill replies:**

A dinner for eight on Saturday with one oven, €120, two vegetarians and one coeliac guest. For this I'd give you
two voices and keep it light: nothing saved, and you can start with them right now. They are fictional, like
characters in a novel.

**A cook and a host, old friends who tease each other.**

**Odile, the cook.** She starts from what is good this week and which dishes belong on the same table, and she
will suggest something you had not considered. She asks: *"What do you want people to remember eating?"* A menu
is right for her when it reads as one meal. Under pressure she adds a course. Ask her what she would cut.

**Bram, the host.** He starts from the evening as your guests will live it: who can eat what, what arrives when,
and where you are standing at 19:40. He asks: *"What are you doing in the twenty minutes before they sit down?"*
A menu is right for him when you sit down with your guests. Under pressure he retreats to dishes you have cooked
ten times. Ask him which single risk is worth taking.

Neither outranks the other. They finish by telling you where they agree, where they differ and what would settle
it, and you decide.

**Odile:** One oven, so let the oven do one beautiful thing. A whole roasted celeriac with brown butter and
hazelnuts feeds the vegetarians as a main, sits beside the lamb for everyone else, and has no gluten in it.
Build the rest cold around it.

**Bram:** I like it if "the rest" is made on Friday. Lamb and celeriac in one oven at two temperatures is where
Saturday goes wrong. Which of them can rest for forty minutes under foil? And the coeliac guest: is the brown
butter thickened with anything, and what goes in the dessert?

**Where they agree:** one oven dish, everything else cold or made ahead, the whole menu gluten-free so nobody
gets a separate plate. **Where they differ:** lamb as well as celeriac (Odile), or celeriac alone with a bigger
cold table (Bram). **What would settle it:** whether your oven holds both trays, and whether your budget covers
lamb for six after the rest is priced.

Want them to price it, or would you like a different pair?
