## Question

[What makes a generated team good](09-what-makes-a-team-good.md) settled how three failures are judged: a persona
flattering the user past a flaw, a real person impersonated, and an irreversible or outward-facing action taken
without the user. It did not settle what the skill does at those moments. Settle: what the team-building skill
says and does when a user insists on a persona of a real person (the default from
[Group metaphors](05-group-metaphors.md) is to encourage fictional names); what is written into a persona or
into `rules.md` so that it does not flatter, and how a persona behaves when the user pushes back without a new
reason; how a working persona recognises that a step is irreversible or outward-facing and stops itself
([Ways to convene](08-ways-to-convene.md) lets personas extend authorised work); and whether any of this differs
between the team-building skill and an emitted team skill.


*Note from [Portraits and visual pages](14-portraits-and-visual-pages.md), 2026-11-14: a request for a portrait that looks like a real person is already settled there (declined, traits offered, no real name or photo in a prompt). This ticket still owns a persona *of* a real person.*


*Note 2026-11-14: version 0.1.0 of the skill assumes, until this ticket decides: a request for a persona of a real person gets one sentence offering a fictional character with the same working habits (`skills/team-building/SKILL.md`); `rules.md` has a section on holding a point until a new reason arrives and a stop list for hard-to-undo steps (`skills/team-building/framework/rules.md`). The same text serves the builder and every team skill.*
